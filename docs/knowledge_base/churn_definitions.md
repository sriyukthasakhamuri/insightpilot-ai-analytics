# Churn Definitions

## Purpose

This document defines the churn-related terms used across InsightPilot analytics, machine learning, and Customer Success workflows.

## Churned Customer

A customer is considered churned when the customer status is recorded as Churned.

The machine-learning target uses:

- `1` = churned
- `0` = not churned

## Churn Probability

Churn probability is the model-estimated likelihood that a customer is at risk of churn.

The probability is generated by the trained churn prediction model and is expressed as a percentage.

Example:

- 0.82 = 82% predicted churn probability
- 0.35 = 35% predicted churn probability

## Predicted Churn

Predicted churn is the binary output generated by the machine-learning model.

- `1` = model predicts churn
- `0` = model predicts no churn

## Risk Tiers

InsightPilot converts churn probability into four operational risk tiers.

### Critical Risk

Churn probability greater than or equal to 80%.

### High Risk

Churn probability from 60% to 79.99%.

### Medium Risk

Churn probability from 40% to 59.99%.

### Low Risk

Churn probability below 40%.

## Model Interpretation

Churn predictions represent statistical risk estimates.

They should not be interpreted as certainty that a customer will churn.

Features associated with higher churn probability should be described as model signals or associations, not as proven causes.

## Retention Priority

Retention priority should consider both:

- predicted churn risk
- potential business impact

Critical and High risk customers should generally receive greater attention, especially when they also represent strategically important customer segments.
