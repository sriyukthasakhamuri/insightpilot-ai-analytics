# Customer Success Playbook

## Purpose

This playbook defines how the Customer Success team should respond to customers based on churn risk, account context, and recent customer experience signals.

## Critical-Risk Workflow

For Critical-risk customers:

- Review the customer profile before outreach.
- Check contract type, payment method, service usage, and recent support history.
- Contact the customer within 24 hours.
- Identify the primary source of dissatisfaction or risk.
- Create a personalized retention action plan.
- Escalate unresolved service issues when needed.
- Record the outreach outcome and next action.

## High-Risk Workflow

For High-risk customers:

- Review recent account and support activity.
- Contact the customer within 3 business days.
- Discuss service concerns, plan fit, and renewal options.
- Offer relevant guidance or retention incentives when justified.
- Schedule follow-up when the issue is not resolved immediately.

## Segment Prioritization

When resources are limited, Customer Success should prioritize:

- Critical-risk customers before High-risk customers.
- High-risk customers before Medium-risk customers.
- Month-to-month customers when they also show elevated churn risk.
- Customers with high churn risk and high business value.

## Outreach Guidance

Customer outreach should be:

- Personalized
- Evidence-based
- Concise
- Focused on solving the customer's actual issue

Avoid generic retention messaging when specific account information is available.

## Recommended Actions by Signal

### Contract Risk

If a customer is on a month-to-month contract and has elevated churn risk:

- Review whether a longer-term contract could better fit their needs.
- Explain relevant contract benefits without applying pressure.

### Payment Risk

If payment behavior appears associated with elevated risk:

- Check for recent billing issues.
- Help the customer resolve payment-related friction.
- Review whether another payment method is more suitable.

### Service Risk

If service-related factors appear associated with elevated risk:

- Review recent service issues.
- Escalate unresolved technical or support problems.
- Offer product education or service optimization when relevant.

## Follow-Up

Every Critical or High-risk intervention should have:

- an owner
- a next action
- a follow-up date
- an outcome status

Customer Success should avoid closing an intervention without documenting the result.
