# Customer Retention Policy

## Purpose

This policy defines how the Customer Success team should respond to customers with elevated churn risk.

## Risk Tiers

### Critical Risk

Customers with a churn probability of 80% or higher are classified as Critical Risk.

Required actions:

- Contact the customer within 24 hours.
- Assign the account to a Customer Success manager.
- Review recent service issues, payment problems, and contract status.
- Offer a personalized retention plan when appropriate.
- Escalate unresolved service complaints.
- Prioritize month-to-month customers when multiple accounts require attention.

### High Risk

Customers with a churn probability between 60% and 79.99% are classified as High Risk.

Required actions:

- Contact the customer within 3 business days.
- Review recent support interactions and service usage.
- Discuss contract renewal or plan optimization.
- Consider retention incentives when justified.

### Medium Risk

Customers with a churn probability between 40% and 59.99% are classified as Medium Risk.

Recommended actions:

- Include the customer in proactive engagement campaigns.
- Monitor changes in service usage and payment behavior.
- Provide educational resources and product guidance.

### Low Risk

Customers with a churn probability below 40% are classified as Low Risk.

Recommended actions:

- Continue standard engagement.
- Monitor for meaningful changes in customer behavior.

## Retention Incentives

Retention incentives must be targeted and should not be offered automatically.

Possible incentives include:

- Temporary account credits
- Plan optimization
- Service upgrades
- Contract renewal incentives

Incentives should be used when they address a documented customer need or retention concern.
