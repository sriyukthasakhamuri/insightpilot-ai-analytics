# Customer Support SLA

## Purpose

This document defines response and escalation expectations for customer issues that may affect retention.

## Critical-Risk Customers

For customers classified as Critical Risk:

- Initial Customer Success outreach should occur within 24 hours.
- Open service issues should be reviewed immediately.
- Unresolved high-impact issues should be escalated to the appropriate support owner.
- A follow-up plan should be documented.

## High-Risk Customers

For customers classified as High Risk:

- Initial outreach should occur within 3 business days.
- Recent service and support activity should be reviewed.
- Important unresolved issues should be prioritized for follow-up.

## Support Priority

Support prioritization should consider:

- churn risk tier
- severity of the customer issue
- account value
- unresolved service problems
- repeated support interactions

## Escalation Guidance

Escalation is appropriate when:

- a Critical-risk customer has an unresolved service issue
- the same issue has required repeated customer contacts
- billing or technical problems remain unresolved
- the issue may materially affect retention

## Documentation Requirements

Every escalated case should include:

- customer ID
- risk tier
- issue summary
- current owner
- escalation reason
- next action
- follow-up date

## Service-Level Interpretation

SLA targets are operational response expectations.

They should be used to guide prioritization and follow-up, not as guarantees of customer retention.
